# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience level, background
  or identity.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique ideas and SEO
  claims, not people.
- No harassment, insults, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, or
  publishing anyone's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic: improving the br-web-seo skill.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/burhanriaz). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code,
and may block repeat offenders.
