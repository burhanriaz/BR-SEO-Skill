# 20-Step Checklist for a High-Performing SEO Article

A content-production checklist for writing/optimizing a single article so it
ranks and gets cited by AI. Pairs with `content-and-geo.md` (method),
`search-intent.md` (intent), `keyword-research.md` (targets) and
`content-strategy.md` (SXO, page verdicts, quality rubric).

1. **Define the primary search intent** — learn, compare, solve, or decide?
2. **Choose one primary topic/keyword** — one clear purpose per page, not many unrelated intents. Check no existing page already targets the same cluster (cannibalization → refresh that page instead).
3. **Map secondary queries & entities** — related questions, subtopics, entities, concepts for full coverage.
4. **Analyze the SERP** — content types, formats, depth, SERP features, dominant intent. If ≥ 6 of the top 10 are one page type, write that type (`content-strategy.md` §A).
5. **Identify competitor content gaps** — unanswered questions, weak/outdated explanations, better-evidence openings.
6. **Add information gain** — original experience, expert insight, examples, data, processes, screenshots — beyond a rewritten SERP.
7. **Build an intent-led outline** — H2/H3 around the reader's questions & journey, not keyword variations.
8. **Answer the main question early** — a useful, direct answer near the top before detail.
9. **Write a search-focused title/H1** — communicate topic & value, incorporate the primary query naturally.
10. **Make sections answer-first** — lead with a concise answer, then explanation, evidence, nuance.
11. **Demonstrate experience & expertise** — first-hand observations, specific examples, expert commentary, original analysis.
12. **Support claims with credible sources** — cite primary/authoritative sources for stats, research, definitions.
13. **Optimize for entities & semantic coverage** — use relevant entities/terminology to clarify relationships.
14. **Add strategic internal links** — to supporting content and commercial pages, descriptive anchors.
15. **Add useful external references** — authoritative links that genuinely help readers verify/understand.
16. **Structure for easy extraction & AI answers** — definitions, concise answer blocks, descriptive headings, lists, tables, comparisons, self-contained sections. Visible Q&A sections are fine; don't expect FAQ/HowTo rich results (Google no longer shows them).
17. **Add purposeful visuals** — original screenshots, diagrams, tables, charts that beat text.
18. **Optimize snippet elements** — strong title tag (~50–60 chars), compelling meta description (~140–160 chars), clean URL, alt text, `Article` structured data with author and `dateModified`.
19. **Add a contextual CTA** — a logical next step for the reader's goal.
20. **Measure, refresh & consolidate** — track queries, rankings, CTR, engagement, conversions, links; after 8–12 weeks assign a KEEP / REFRESH / CONSOLIDATE / REMOVE verdict (`content-strategy.md` §B) on evidence.

**Pre-publish gate:** score the draft with the E-E-A-T rubric in
`content-strategy.md` §D. Publish at ≥ 70 with no hard vetoes; any veto (e.g.
YMYL without a named qualified reviewer, a material factual error, unreviewed AI
text) blocks publishing regardless of score.
