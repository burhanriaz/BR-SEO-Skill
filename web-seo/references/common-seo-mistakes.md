# Costly SEO Mistakes (and fixes)

The 8 most expensive mistakes — each with its business cost and the priority
fix. Use as a fast triage lens during an audit: these are high-impact, so they
usually rank as 🔴/🟠 findings.

| # | Mistake | What it costs (business impact) | Priority fix |
|---|---------|-------------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Targeting the wrong search intent** | Traffic without customers; attracts non-buyers, wasted spend | Align to intent — analyze SERPs, match content to each buyer-journey stage (see `search-intent.md`) |
| 2 | **Ignoring indexability & crawl issues** | Valuable pages blocked, re-indexed late, or undiscoverable | Fix technical barriers — audit crawl access, robots directives, canonicals, internal links |
| 3 | **Publishing overlapping / competing content** | Fragmented visibility; cannibalization dilutes relevance | Consolidate or differentiate — merge, or clearly distinguish each page's intent |
| 4 | **Weak internal linking** | Even strong pages under-rank and get too little traffic | Build strategic internal links from relevant, high-authority pages with descriptive anchors (see `internal-linking.md`) |
| 5 | **Neglecting high-intent commercial pages** | Money/service pages lose to competitors; mixed leads & revenue | Optimize money pages — clear value props, credibility, internal links |
| 6 | **Ignoring content decay** | Declining organic performance as content ages | Refresh strategically — update info, add depth/relevance, strengthen internal links |
| 7 | **Poor conversion optimization** | Lost customers; visitors leave without acting | Improve the conversion path — stronger CTAs, UX, trust signals, less friction |
| 8 | **Measuring rankings instead of revenue** | Misallocated time & budget chasing vanity rankings | Track business outcomes — leads, conversions, revenue alongside rankings/impressions |

## How to apply

Run this list early in an audit as a triage filter, then record concrete
instances as findings with evidence in the audit report. Mistakes 1–4 are usually
blockers/high; 5–8 are high/medium depending on the site's goals.
